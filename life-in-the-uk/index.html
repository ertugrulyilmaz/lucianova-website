Life in the UK

Pass the Life in the UK Test on your first attempt.

Coming soon to iOS and Android

Life in the UK Test 2026 helps you prepare for the official Life in the UK Test, the citizenship and settlement (ILR) test set by the UK Home Office.

Practise questions, sit full mock tests and track your progress towards your test date. The app works offline and needs no account: your answers and progress stay on your phone. Premium is a one-off purchase, not a subscription.

This app is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with the Home Office or the UK Government. The only official place to book the test is gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Lucia Nova Creative Ltd ("we", "us", "our") operates the website lucianovacreative.com. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this site, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

What we collect

This website does not use contact forms, sign-up flows, or cookies beyond what your browser automatically provides when loading any web page. When you visit lucianovacreative.com, our web hosting provider records standard server access logs, which may include your IP address, browser type, referring URL, and the pages you requested. This is a normal function of all web servers and is outside our direct control.

If you send us an email at contact@lucianovacreative.com, we will receive and store your email address and the contents of your message in order to respond to you. We do not share this information with third parties, and we retain it only as long as necessary to handle your enquiry.

What we do not collect

We do not use tracking pixels, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics on this website. We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Our apps

Lucia Nova Creative publishes apps on the Apple App Store and Google Play. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its respective store listing. The privacy practices of our apps are separate from this website policy.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google may log your IP address in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. No other third-party services are used on this site.

Your rights

If you are located in the UK or European Economic Area, you have rights under data protection law including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@lucianovacreative.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Curio

A few minutes of curiosity, every day.

Coming soon to iOS and Android

Curio is a daily card app for intellectually curious people. Word origins, mythology, philosophy and lost history, written to be read in about sixty seconds and remembered for longer.

Save the cards you want to keep and come back to them whenever you like. Curio has no ads and does not sell your data.

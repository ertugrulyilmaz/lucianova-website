How do I restore a purchase?

Premium is a one-off purchase, not a subscription, and it's tied to your Google Play or App Store account rather than a login. Open the app and go to Settings → Restore Purchase, then make sure you're signed into the same Google Play or Apple ID account used to buy it. If it still doesn't unlock, email us the order receipt from Google Play or the App Store and we'll sort it out.

I found a bug or incorrect question

Email us a description and, if you can, a screenshot. We check the official Home Office source material whenever a question is reported.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Practice questions and mock tests all work offline. The app only goes online briefly to check for content updates and to verify a purchase.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Your progress is stored on your device, not in an account, so it doesn't carry over automatically. Premium can always be restored on a new device — see "How do I restore a purchase?" above.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your device. There's no account or server-side data to remove. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is this app affiliated with the Home Office?

No. This is an independent study aid, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Home Office or the UK Government. The only official place to book the Life in the UK Test is gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test.

Still need help?

Email support@lucianovacreative.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days.

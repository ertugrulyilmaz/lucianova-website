Life in the UK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what the Life in the UK Test Prep app collects and why. The app is published by Lucia Nova Creative Ltd ("we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17228822), registered office 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom. We are the data controller for the information described here.

The short version

  • Your study data stays on your phone. There is no account and no sign-in, and your answers are never uploaded.
  • The app counts anonymous usage, such as when it is opened, to help us improve it.
  • There is no advertising, and nothing is sold.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device.

What stays on your device

Your answers, mock results, progress, test date and settings are kept in the app’s own storage on your phone, along with a copy of the study content so the app works offline.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

No account

You never sign up or sign in, and the app never asks for your name, email address or phone number. Your progress lives on this device only, so a new phone starts empty, but a purchase can always be restored from the app store.

What the app sends

The app sends anonymous usage statistics, such as when it is opened, so we can see how it is used. They are not linked to your name, your email address or any account, and they never include your answers.

Premium is a one-off purchase through Google Play or the App Store. The store handles the payment, and we never see your card or billing details. A purchase service keeps track of whether premium is unlocked, so that it can be restored.

When you are online, the app downloads corrections to the study content. This only downloads content: nothing about you or your progress is uploaded.

Service providers

We use a small number of service providers for usage statistics, purchases and study content. They work on our behalf, receive only what they need for that job, and some may be based outside the UK.

We do not sell data or share it for advertising or marketing, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites.

Security and retention

Every connection the app makes is encrypted. Your study data stays on your device until you reset your progress or delete the app. Usage statistics and purchase records are kept only as long as they are needed to run and improve the app, or as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

Settings → Reset progress deletes your answers, mock results and quiz tallies. Deleting the app removes everything else it stored.

You can ask us about any data related to your use of the app, or ask us to delete it, by writing to support@lucianovacreative.com. If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for the Life in the UK Test and is not directed at children.

This app is not the Home Office

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Home Office or the UK Government. It is an independent study aid. The Life in the UK Test itself is a government service — the only official place to book it is gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the app, and change the date at the top.

This app is published by Lucia Nova Creative LTD. Questions about privacy go to support@lucianovacreative.com.

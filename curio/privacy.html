Curio

Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 June 2026

Curio is built around a simple idea: one beautiful card a day. Our approach to your data is just as restrained.

This policy explains what Curio collects, why, and the choices you have. Curio is operated by Lucia Nova Creative Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the information described here.

The short version. We do not sell your data. We do not build advertising profiles. We collect the minimum needed to run your account, sync what you save, and understand which parts of the app work — and the usage analytics we do collect are anonymous and can be switched off at any time.

1. Information we collect

We collect three kinds of information, and no more than these:

  • Account information. When you sign in, we store the email address associated with your account so we can authenticate you and sync your saved cards across devices. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a basic account identifier (and, depending on your settings, an email address) from that provider to create your account.
  • Content you create in the app. The cards you bookmark and the collections you build are stored against your account so they are available whenever you sign in.
  • Anonymous usage analytics. To understand how Curio is used — for example, which card categories people read to the end — we record anonymous activity events such as “a card was viewed” or “a collection was created”. These events are not linked to your name or email address. They are tied only to a random identifier generated on your device.

We deliberately do not collect your name, contacts, location, photos, advertising identifiers, or any other personal data Curio does not need to function.

2. Payment information

Curio offers a paid subscription. All payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms. We never see or store your card number or billing details. We receive only confirmation of your subscription status so we can unlock paid features.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • create and secure your account and sign you in;
  • sync your bookmarks and collections across your devices;
  • deliver the daily card and, if you enable them, daily reminder notifications;
  • understand, in aggregate and anonymously, how the app is used so we can improve it;
  • maintain reliability and diagnose technical problems.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

4. Legal bases (UK GDPR)

Where UK data protection law applies, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract — to provide the account, sync, and subscription features you ask for.
  • Consent — for daily notifications and for anonymous usage analytics, both of which you can turn off at any time in Settings.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the service secure and working reliably, balanced against your rights.

5. Where your data is stored

Account data — your email and the cards and collections you save — is stored on our infrastructure provider Supabase, on servers located in the United Kingdom.

Anonymous usage analytics are processed by PostHog using European Union hosting. Because these analytics are not tied to your identity, they do not reveal who you are.

Most of your in-app preferences, such as your chosen theme, are kept on your device.

6. Sharing with others

We share information only with the service providers that make Curio work, and only as needed:

  • Supabase — account, authentication, and synced content storage (UK).
  • PostHog — anonymous product analytics (EU).
  • Apple and Google — sign-in and subscription processing.

Each of these acts as our processor under a data processing agreement, or as an independent controller for the parts they operate (such as payment). We may also disclose information if required by law.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account information and saved content for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete the associated account data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited records to meet legal obligations. Anonymous analytics events are retained in aggregate and are not linked to you.

8. Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. You can exercise these rights, including deleting your account, by contacting us at the address below.

You also have the right to complain to the UK’s Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

9. Your choices in the app

  • Analytics. You can switch off anonymous usage analytics at any time in Settings; no further events will be recorded.
  • Notifications. Daily reminders are off until you turn them on, and can be disabled at any time.
  • Your account. You can sign out, or request deletion of your account and its data.

10. Children

Curio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Curio evolves. When we make material changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

12. Contact

For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, write to us at contact@lucianovacreative.com.

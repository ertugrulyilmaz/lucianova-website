Life in the UK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy explains what the Life in the UK app collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is published by Lucia Nova Creative LTD, a company registered in England and Wales (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the information described here.

The short version

  • Your study data stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no copy of your answers on any server.
  • Nothing is sold, and there is no advertising in the app.
  • Three services see something: an analytics service is told when the app opens and, once, the first time your practice reaches “test-ready”; the purchase service is told what you bought when you buy or restore; and the server that supplies the questions sees your network address, as any website would.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps a database in its own private storage on your phone. It holds:

  • your mock exam attempts: which paper, when you started and finished, your score and the number of questions;
  • every answer you have given: which question, what you chose, whether it was right, and a copy of the question and the option you picked as they were worded at the time;
  • a running tally of answered and correct questions for each unit;
  • your settings: whether you have finished the introduction, the test date you entered, whether you chose light or dark, and when you first reached “test-ready”;
  • the app store’s last answer about your purchase: whether it is active, whether it never expires, when it runs out and when it was last checked.

It also keeps a copy of the study content itself (the chapters, units, questions, mock papers, timeline and key figures) so the app works with no connection at all.

None of this leaves your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

There is no account

You are never asked to sign up, sign in, or give a name, an email address or a phone number. There is no password to forget and no profile to look up.

That also means there is no backup. Your progress lives on this device only, so a new phone starts empty. A purchase can still be restored, because the app store remembers it.

Analytics

The app uses PostHog to count how it is used. The app itself sends two events:

  • app_opened, once each time the app starts;
  • test_ready_reached, the first time (and only the first time) your practice reaches “test-ready”. It carries the average score that earned it and nothing else.

The app never tells PostHog who you are. PostHog knows this installation only by a random identifier its own software generates. Nothing about your answers, your scores, your test date or your purchase is sent to it.

PostHog’s software, on its standard settings, also records app lifecycle moments on its own (the app being installed, opened, updated and put into the background) and attaches technical context to every event, such as your device model, operating system version and app version. It contacts PostHog when the app starts to fetch its configuration. Screen recording and session replay are switched off.

Like any server, PostHog receives the network (IP) address each request comes from, and may use it to estimate a rough location such as your country or city. PostHog stores this data in the European Union.

Purchases

Premium is a one-off purchase through Google Play or Apple’s App Store: you pay once and nothing renews. The store takes the payment and holds the billing relationship. The app never sees or handles your card, and is never told your name, your email address or your billing address.

The app uses RevenueCat to keep track of what the store says you own. RevenueCat is set up each time the app starts and identifies this installation by a random identifier it generates. When you buy or restore, it records the purchase and whether premium is active, against the store account that made it. It is never sent your study data. RevenueCat is based in the United States and processes this data there.

Where the questions come from

The study content ships inside the app and is topped up from Supabase when a connection is available. That connection is read-only: the app asks for published content with a public key and never writes anything back. No progress, no answers, no settings.

As with any request to any website, that server can see the network address the request came from, and when it was made. The server is in the European Union (Ireland).

If you email us

If you write to us, for example to report a question you think is wrong, we receive your email address and whatever your message contains. We use them only to reply to you and act on what you asked.

What the app does not do

There is no advertising, and the app does not use your device’s advertising ID. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing. There are no third-party trackers beyond the services named in this policy, and no attempt is made to follow you between apps or websites.

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Who we share data with

We use these service providers, and each receives only what this policy describes:

  • PostHog: product analytics (European Union).
  • RevenueCat: purchase records (United States).
  • Supabase: delivering study content (European Union).
  • Google and Apple: payments through Google Play and the App Store, under their own privacy policies.

PostHog, RevenueCat and Supabase process data on our behalf. Where data leaves the UK, as with RevenueCat in the United States, we rely on the safeguards UK data protection law requires for international transfers. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How your data is protected

Every connection the app makes to these services is encrypted with HTTPS. Your study data stays in the app’s private storage on your device, where other apps cannot read it, and we hold no copy of it. Access to our accounts with the providers above is limited to the people who build and run the app.

How long it is kept

  • On your device: until you use Reset progress or delete the app.
  • Analytics events: for as long as they help us understand how the app is used, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: for as long as they are needed to confirm and restore your purchase, and to keep the financial records the law requires.
  • Content requests: Supabase keeps request logs for a short time to run and secure the service.
  • Emails: for as long as it takes to deal with your message.

Legal bases

Under UK data protection law, we rely on:

  • Legitimate interests for analytics, to understand in aggregate how the app is used so we can improve it; for content requests, to deliver study content and keep the service secure; and for replying to messages you send us.
  • Contract for purchases, to give you the premium access you bought.

Your choices in the app

  • Reset progress (in Settings) deletes every answer, mock result and unit quiz tally on the device, which also empties the Mistake Bank. It deliberately keeps your purchase, your test date and your display settings.
  • Deleting the app removes everything else it stored. Because there is no account, there is nothing on our side to look up or erase for your study data.
  • Restore purchases on the upgrade screen brings premium back on a new device, signed in to the store account you bought with.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to data portability. To use any of these rights, email support@lucianovacreative.com.

Because there is no account, the records PostHog and RevenueCat hold are tied to a random identifier, not to your name. To find yours, we may need your store order number, from the receipt Google Play or Apple sent you.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Children

The app is meant for adults preparing for the Life in the UK Test and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

This app is not the Home Office

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Home Office or the UK Government. It is an independent study aid. The Life in the UK Test itself is a government service — the only official place to book it is gov.uk/life-in-the-uk-test.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the copy in the app, and change the date at the top.

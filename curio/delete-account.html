Delete Your Account

Curio, by Lucia Nova Creative Ltd

You can delete your Curio account and its data at any time. Here's how.

This page explains how to request deletion of your Curio account, operated by Lucia Nova Creative Ltd (company number 17228822). You can do this from inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Curio and go to Settings.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm by typing DELETE when prompted.

Your account and associated data are removed immediately once you confirm.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you've already removed the app, email us from the address linked to your Curio account at contact@lucianovacreative.com with the subject line “Delete my account”. We'll verify the request and delete your account within 30 days, and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

DataWhat happens
Your account & email addressPermanently deleted
Your bookmarked cardsPermanently deleted
Your collectionsPermanently deleted
In-app preferences (theme, etc.)Removed from your device

What we keep, and for how long

We retain only what we're legally required to, and only for as long as necessary:

  • Anonymous usage analytics. Curio records anonymous activity events (for example, that a card was viewed) that are never linked to your name, email, or account. Because this data cannot be tied back to you, it is not removed by an account-deletion request, and it does not identify you.
  • Limited legal/transaction records. Where the law requires it — for example, records relating to a subscription purchase — we may keep minimal records for the period required by UK law, after which they are deleted. Subscription payments themselves are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Note on subscriptions. Deleting your Curio account does not automatically cancel an active subscription billed through Apple or Google. To stop future charges, also cancel the subscription in your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings.

Questions

If you need help with deletion, contact us at contact@lucianovacreative.com.

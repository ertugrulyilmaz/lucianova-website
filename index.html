About

Small studio.
Deliberate work.

Lucia Nova Creative is an independent app studio. We build iOS and Android apps that prioritise depth over noise — products designed to last, not to trend. Every app we make starts from a genuine curiosity about what it could be, not what already exists.

We work across the full product lifecycle: concept, design, development, and launch. Our process is unhurried by necessity — we take on few projects so that each one gets the attention it deserves.

Lucia Nova Creative Ltd is registered in England and Wales. We are based in London.

Enquiries: contact@lucianovacreative.com
